A quantum-safe transaction, without the weight.

Post-quantum signatures are 10 to 120 times bigger than ECDSA, so putting them on chain would cut Monad's block capacity by up to 90%. Sigpress puts one hash-based proof on chain instead of the signatures, so the signature stops being the transaction.

Bytes on chain per operation: 23x smaller, measured on testnet today.

The cliff

Monad rations bytes. Post-quantum signatures spend the whole budget.

Transactions a 1.5 MB block can hold (MIP-12 capacity model, not a live-TPS claim). ML-DSA-44 is the scheme in Monad's own account-auth draft. Sigpress returns capacity to the transaction-count ceiling by keeping signatures off chain.

Where it stands

The validity proof already runs end to end on our own signatures. What's left is hardening, not feasibility.

    Credit: the aggregation idea is EIP-8288's (Buterin & Coratger), proposed for Ethereum's protocol and not working anywhere yet. Sigpress is a working app-layer implementation of that design on Monad, with the on-chain verifier it only specifies.
